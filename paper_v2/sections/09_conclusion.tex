\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
%======================================================================

AEIC-MD treats structured AEIC reports as the observable interface of a multi-agent mechanism.  In paired formal experiments, a public Static-AEIC rule substantially raises both revealed high-effort behavior and decision quality, whereas outcome-only reward does not improve quality.  Reward--cost responses, controlled information conditions, seeds, and panel-size tests support the behavioral interpretation.  At the same time, the negative GLM replication limits the claim to the tested backend and makes contract calibration across model families the central next step.  The appropriate main story is therefore mechanism-mediated follower response---with topology as a subsequent information-control layer---not topology optimization alone.
