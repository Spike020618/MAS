\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
%======================================================================

We subjected a game-theoretic multi-agent consensus framework---AEIC decomposition, a three-layer Stackelberg mechanism, and adaptive protocol routing---to a diagnose-then-intervene validation on automated scholarly review. The diagnosis is unambiguous: LLM review panels herd. Independent reviews carry a systematic reject bias (14.4\% accept rate on clear-accept papers); unstructured discussion triggers a negative ratchet that eliminates 78\% of minority positions with flips running 96:0 toward rejection ($p{=}2.5{\times}10^{-29}$), collapses rating variance by 51\% ($p{=}3.9{\times}10^{-32}$), and never improves decision agreement. Herding is universal across three model families, but its direction is model-dependent---DeepSeek-V3 and R1 ratchet toward rejection while GLM-4-Flash ratchets toward acceptance---and the strongest reasoner suffers the largest diversity collapse: capability does not protect against herding, it selects the attractor.

The interventions map mechanism class to pathology. Aggregation-time calibration, discovered autonomously by Stackelberg optimization as a $+1.04$ bias term, repairs the reject bias (clear-accept agreement 2\%$\to$38\%)---and a re-thresholded mean matches it, so \emph{calibrate first, in whatever form}. Prompt-level behavioral regulation cannot break the ratchet (elimination 78\%$\to$71\%) though it preserves decision quality: rhetoric is no match for a conformist prior, and effective regulation must act on mechanism or structure. Per-paper routing matches the best fixed protocol at 56\% lower cost, demoting deliberation to an on-demand resource. Throughout, behavioral health metrics proved orders of magnitude more sensitive than decision accuracy; we advocate minority survival, variance preservation, and drift as primary endpoints for any multi-agent judgment system, with accuracy demoted to a safety constraint.

Future work includes mechanism-level regulation that protects minorities at aggregation time rather than in prompts, heterogeneous panels mixing model families (whose opposite attractors may cancel), richer prospective features to close the 10pp oracle gap in routing, and transfer of the deliberation health metrics to production multi-agent pipelines---recommendation ensembles, multi-critic evaluation, and agentic systems that deliberate before acting---as observability signals.
