\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
%======================================================================

AEIC-MD turns structured reports into an observable interface for leader--follower experimentation. A public Static-AEIC rule substantially changes the primary backend's effort choices and improves collective accuracy, whereas an outcome-only reward does not. Dose response, information controls, robustness suites, and frozen provenance support this bounded interpretation. The held-out-backend failure is equally important: effective contracts must be calibrated to the model policy that interprets them. The resulting research direction is not ever-more elaborate topology optimization, but a disciplined sequence of observable effort, calibrated incentives, and only then adaptive information flow.
